# Parang Ludwig — Card Texts (Writer + Editor output)

## Concept

Parang Ludwig · Long of Edge/Moth/Grail · Lionsmith ascension. Once an apprentice of the tribal shamans. The year the colonists destroyed the shrine, spirits dismembered him in a near-death vision — and the reassembly left a shard of iron inside him. Not crystal. A shard left over from the shattered icon. Three alchemical transformations followed: the nigredo, when the old self came away from his body; the tower's fall, when he split the watchtower with the blade forged from that iron; the rubedo, when the reassembly completed and he was no longer a man. After ascension he became a ghost of the rainforest.

---

### 1. The Parang (Tool · Edge, Grail, Tool)
> Three weld-lines run down the blade. The first is blue-black, thumb-wide — it broke the year the old self came away. The second has paled to iron-grey and narrowed by half; the edge curled when it opened the watchtower's iron door. The third shows only against the light. Steel that has broken and been forged back is denser than before. Stand beside a man who has broken three times, and the hardest bone in your body will hum.

### 2. The Forge Clearing (Location · Edge, Location)
> A vine-covered forge of cut stone stands in the forest. Its mortar matches nothing in the colonial building records. Two hundred years old at least. The dents on the anvil are narrower than hammer-marks — narrower than any tool made for forging. Not one tree in the ring around the clearing is older than a hundred and twenty; the youngest broke ground seven years ago. Three feet down, diggers turned up half a blade-tip, edge upward, no hilt. It went into the earth forty years before the forge.

### 3. The Lionsmith's Lesson (Lore · Edge, Lore)
> The Lionsmith broke his sword. Seven pieces of iron. Forged anew. Quenched anew. Broken anew. Seven times over, until the pieces no longer shone and the edge no longer curled. The note on the final page is no part of the original text — tropical hardwood charcoal, in Malay. Translation: The first breaking was done to me. All the breakings after were my own.

### 4. The Split Watchtower (Location · Edge, Location)
> The ruin of a stone watchtower. It was not brought down by blast — it was split from crest to base, as clean as a cut through steel too hot to resist. A splinter of iron the size of a fingernail is set in the mortar at the base. The assay report says the metal belongs to no known lode. The banyans around the tower have grown toward the split; their roots reach into it. As if filling a wound.

### 5. The Book of Transformations (Book · Edge, Moth, Book)
> Bark paper, charcoal. It records the three transformations in every detail — the bones the spirits counted as they took him apart; the first strip of skin the old self shed in the nigredo; the precise angle at which the edge curled against the watchtower door. The last entry is dated the night before the rubedo. The hand is no longer a man's.

### 6. The Recitation of the Blade (Lore · Edge, Lore)
> Written in the shamans' script. The invaders' archives do not hold this one. At the second line of the recitation, rifles misfire. At the fourth, charts cannot be read. At the seventh, compass needles turn on their own. At the third canto, from deep in the rainforest, comes the sound of blades — one stroke to every syllable, keeping time with the chant. When the last line ends, the sound stops. There is no need to speak it again. The machete is already at the door.

### 7. The Rainforest Dream (Dream · Moth)
> A dream has caught the man who wanders the jungle's edge: he moves among the trees without a word, and squats at the buttress roots, cutting signs with a blade. Every night the shapes come out wrong. The first night he woke with his palms soaked. By the third night he was copying the dream's remnants in the dark: three slanting strokes that tear straight through, two arcs that return upon themselves, one angle as final as a bone breaking.

### 8. The Rite of the Forged Bone (Rite · Edge, Grail, Rite)
> Open the left side with the blade, half an inch from the old scar of the initiation. Paint the first weld-line with blood from the fingertip; repeat three times. At the third anointing the blade will go briefly transparent — an icon-shard is fixed in the iron. The shard is still hot.
